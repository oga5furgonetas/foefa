FDS is the app for couriers who work for themselves: your route in order, turn-by-turn navigation, customers kept in the loop and your earnings, all on your phone and with no paper sheets.

WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU
• Scan each parcel's label (barcode or photo) and the stop goes in by itself: address, phone and number of parcels.
• Orders your route by real driving time, with the typical traffic of each hour and each customer's opening hours.
• Navigate stop by stop with voice guidance, inside the app or with Google Maps and Waze.
• Call or WhatsApp the customer with one tap; the app records when you arrived, called and how long you waited.
• The customer gets a link and answers you: whether they'll be home, where to leave it, the door code.
• Proof of delivery with photo, signature and who received it; cash on delivery in your local currency.
• Where did I park: mark the van and walk back to it without wandering.
• My money today: what you've earned so far, fuel and what each failed delivery costs you.
• Works without signal: changes are saved on the phone and sent by themselves when the network is back.

WHO IT'S FOR
Independent couriers and small delivery businesses: parcels, food, pharmacy, flowers, furniture… anyone who goes out with a van or a car and has to deliver in order.

PRICE
Free and unlimited until December 31, 2026, no card needed. After that, free up to 15 stops a day or unlimited with the Courier plan, priced in your local currency. Cancel anytime from the app.

Also on the web: the same account works on your phone and your computer.
